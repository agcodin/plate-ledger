      How the numbers are built. Every food carries calories, protein, carbohydrate, fat and fiber per 100 g; your amount is converted to grams and scaled. Indian and South Indian dishes are entered as they are normally cooked at home — sambar with its usual tempering, dosa off a greased tawa, curries with their oil — so a restaurant version will run richer. Values are reference figures, close enough to steer a deficit, not a lab assay. Alcohol carries calories that belong to no macro, so a drink adds to the calorie total without a matching protein, carbohydrate or fat share. Weight-change estimates use the conventional 3,500 kcal per pound of body fat.
